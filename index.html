Transit & routes from 21st Ave & Judah St

The real geography of your commute, so you can weigh each opportunity.

Your home is at 21st Avenue & Judah Street, Inner Sunset (SF 94122). Judah Street is the spine of the N Judah Muni Metro, which runs directly through your neighborhood into downtown San Francisco and on to the Caltrain/Embarcadero end. The fastest trips are along this rail corridor and the west-side bus lines. Travel times below are directional, not measured — confirm live schedules with SFMTA / 511 / Google Transit before applying.

Verification & anti-scam checklist

The rules used to include every employer on this page.

  • No hallucinated entries. Every employer was checked against its official website and a verifiable physical SF address this session; source links are shown on each card for manual review.
  • Verified / real company only. I excluded anything I could not confirm with an official site or real registered business/location.
  • Apply directly with the employer — via its own careers portal, application URL, or listed email. No recruiters, no third-party job boards as the application channel.
  • Recruiters/staffing aggregators flagged: if a listing was only reachable through a recruiter or 3rd-party "easy apply" site, it was treated as discovery-only or dropped.
  • Flag for review: items with caveats (licensure needed, long commute, internal-only posting, out-of-district) are explicitly flagged rather than hidden.
  • Live re-verification is on you: postings rotate daily. Re-open the employer's official careers page at application time.

Red flags to reject

  • Asking for money, "payment for training," or a fee to apply.
  • Asking for banking/SSN/personal financial info before an interview.
  • Vague email-only contact, no verifiable website, or a mismatched domain (e.g., "hiring@company-support.xyz").
  • Recruiter-only channels where the end employer cannot be identified and verified.
  • Guaranteed money with no real company/lab site.
